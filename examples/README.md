# Examples

One folder per TV model, with debloat lists or firmware-specific launcher instructions.
Debloat lists are starting points: comment out anything you use.

| Folder | TV | OS | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `xiaomi-mi-tv-4a/` | Xiaomi Mi TV 4A (MiTV-MOOQ1) | Android TV 10 | PatchWall, Mi analytics, SmartShare, MiLink |
| [`tcl-c6k/`](tcl-c6k/README.md) | TCL C6K (G08) | Google TV / Android 14 | Personal dock, Portuguese UI, installation and restore scripts; Google TV recovery workaround |

To add your TV, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). The quickest way to get a list is to
run the prompt in [PROMPT.md](../PROMPT.md) and copy the packages your assistant disabled.
